सफ़र

A Life in Fragments

١٩٢١

Ludhiana

Abdul Hayee — born into privilege and torn from it. A young man who watched his mother fight for custody, who turned poverty into poetry and the street into his classroom.

जन्म · Origin
١٩٤٣

Talkhiyaan

His debut collection — तल्ख़ियाँ — bitter truths wrapped in verse. The literary world noticed. Faiz Ahmed Faiz called him a voice of a generation.

शायरी · First Book
١٩٤٩

Bombay

Chased by warrants for sedition in Lahore, he fled to Bombay — a city that would make him immortal. The Film Industry. Guru Dutt. SD Burman. A new canvas.

प्रवास · Bombay
١٩٥٧

Pyaasa

ये महलों, ये तख़्तों, ये ताजों की दुनिया… Pyaasa became a mirror held to society. Sahir became the conscience of Indian cinema.

सिनेमा · Cinema
١٩٦٣

Taj Mahal

He became the first lyricist to demand equal royalty as composers. The Taj Mahal controversy — a poet who owned his words, in an era that didn't let poets own anything.

विद्रोह · Rebellion
١٩٨٠

Silence

25 October 1980. Heart failure in Bombay. He had said: मैं पल दो पल का शायर हूँ। He was wrong. He was forever.
